🔒 About privacy

Only signed-in members can view the directory and feed. Private messages are restricted to their two participants after a friend request is accepted.

Blocking stops new friend requests and messages between two users. Reported accounts are sent privately to the site owner for manual review. This is a basic safety tool, not automatic moderation; feed posts remain visible to signed-in members.

Your account password is handled by Firebase Authentication. Never share it.

🚩 Report user

Reports are private and reviewed manually. Reporting does not automatically ban someone. If you feel unsafe, you can also block this account.
