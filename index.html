🔒 About privacy

Only signed-in members can search display names and bios. Your posts are visible only to you and accepted friends under the matching Firestore rules. Private messages are restricted to approved friends.

Blocking stops new friend requests and messages between two users. Reported accounts are sent privately to the site owner for manual review. This is a basic safety tool, not automatic moderation; friend-only posts require the updated Firestore rules; older website versions cannot load the full feed.

Your account password is handled by Firebase Authentication. Never share it.

🔔 Incoming call alerts

Get a browser notification when someone calls while My Social is open in another tab. The site must remain open. Alerts when the site is fully closed need a future push-notification setup.
